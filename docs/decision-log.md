# Decision log

This log records meaningful product and engineering decisions. It captures decision-quality evidence rather than private stream-of-consciousness reasoning.

## 2026-11-11 — Publish selected project milestones

**Status:** Accepted

**Problem**

The finished application alone will not show how product and engineering decisions evolved or how evidence changed the work.

**Options considered**

1. Publish only a final case study, creating a concise narrative but losing intermediate decision evidence.
2. Publish every development update, creating a detailed record with substantial noise and maintenance cost.
3. Publish selected evidence-rich milestones, balancing continuity with editorial quality.

**Choice**

Publish articles for selected product and engineering milestones on brianshimkus.com/blog.

**Rationale**

Milestone posts can demonstrate product judgment, technical tradeoffs, validation, and learning while supporting the final case study and interview preparation.

**Tradeoffs and risks**

Writing takes time away from implementation, and premature posts could preserve weak conclusions. A checkpoint must contain validated evidence or a meaningful decision before it becomes a publication candidate.

**Reconsideration trigger**

Revisit the cadence if writing delays vertical slices or if milestones do not produce distinct evidence.

**Ownership**

Brian chose to document appropriate checkpoints on his website. Drafting support may be assisted, but Brian reviews and approves every article before publication.
