# Decision log

This log records meaningful product and engineering decisions. It captures decision-quality evidence rather than private stream-of-consciousness reasoning.

## Decision template

### YYYY-MM-DD — Decision title

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Reconsidered | Superseded

**Problem**

What decision is required, and why does it matter now?

**Options considered**

1. Option and its relevant strengths and weaknesses.
2. Option and its relevant strengths and weaknesses.

**Choice**

What did we decide?

**Rationale**

What evidence, assumptions, and constraints support the choice?

**Tradeoffs and risks**

What becomes harder, less flexible, or less certain?

**Reconsideration trigger**

What new evidence or condition would cause us to revisit this decision?

**Ownership**

What did Brian decide or implement, and what assistance supported the work?
