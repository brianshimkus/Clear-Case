# Interview evidence log

This log converts completed, verifiable project work into concise interview evidence. Entries must distinguish portfolio demonstrations from production experience and accurately state Brian's individual contribution.

## Evidence template

### Milestone or decision

**Interview theme:** Product judgment | Front-end architecture | Applied AI | Accessibility | Testing | Security | Collaboration

**Situation**

What user or engineering problem existed?

**Task**

What was Brian responsible for deciding or delivering?

**Action**

What did Brian personally decide, design, implement, test, or validate? What assistance was used?

**Result**

What observable outcome or evidence resulted? Use measured results when available.

**Tradeoff**

What alternative was rejected, and why?

**Likely follow-up questions**

- What evidence informed the decision?
- What failed or changed during implementation?
- How would this approach change in a production environment?
- What would you measure next?

**Claim boundary**

State clearly whether the evidence comes from production employment, independent client work, or this portfolio project.
