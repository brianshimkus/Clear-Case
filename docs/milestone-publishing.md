# Milestone publishing

Clear Case will use selected project milestones as source material for articles on [brianshimkus.com/blog](https://www.brianshimkus.com/blog).

## Publishing standard

A checkpoint is worth publishing when it contains evidence of a meaningful product or engineering decision. Posts should explain:

- The user problem and why it matters
- The options considered
- The decision and tradeoffs
- What was designed, implemented, or tested
- What the evidence showed
- What changed as a result
- What remains uncertain

Posts should not be routine progress reports or feature inventories. They should demonstrate how Brian makes complex workflows clear, usable, and reliable.

## Candidate checkpoints

1. Product framing: narrowing the user, job to be done, risky assumptions, and outcome
2. Workflow and domain model: cases, exceptions, evidence, decisions, roles, and audit events
3. First vertical slice: an exception moving from queue to evidence-backed resolution
4. Responsible AI: grounding, correction, approval, and failure handling
5. Product proof: usability findings, accessibility results, critical-flow tests, and resulting changes
6. Final case study: outcomes, limitations, and lessons for production use

## Draft workflow

1. Complete and validate the milestone.
2. Update the decision and interview-evidence logs.
3. Draft the article from verified project evidence.
4. Review claims, screenshots, fictional data, accessibility, and links.
5. Publish only after Brian explicitly approves the final draft.

## Claim boundaries

- Identify Clear Case as an independent portfolio project.
- Separate implemented behavior from proposed future work.
- Separate test results from assumptions and hypotheses.
- Do not use confidential employer or client information.
- Do not imply production AI operations, model training, or ML research.
