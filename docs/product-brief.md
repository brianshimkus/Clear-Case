# Product brief

## Product concept

Clear Case is an AI-assisted exception-management workspace for complex onboarding workflows.

## Purpose

Clear Case helps operations teams resolve incomplete or conflicting onboarding cases with less manual investigation and clearer accountability. It brings application data, PDF evidence, business rules, suggested next steps, human decisions, and audit history into one accessible workspace.

## Problem

Complex onboarding work is often spread across forms, PDF documents, email, spreadsheets, and disconnected systems. Operations specialists must find missing information, compare conflicting values, interpret requirements, contact applicants, and document every decision.

This creates four recurring problems:

- Reviewers spend too much time gathering and reconciling information.
- Important exceptions can be overlooked or handled inconsistently.
- Applicants receive slow or unclear requests for missing information.
- Teams lack a reliable record of the evidence and reasoning behind decisions.

## Proposed solution

Clear Case turns each application into a structured, reviewable case. The product applies deterministic validation rules, identifies missing or conflicting information, and uses AI to summarize evidence and draft recommended next steps. Every suggestion links back to its source. A human reviewer remains responsible for correcting, approving, rejecting, and completing the case.

## Initial scope

- Operations queue with ownership, status, age, and exception filters
- Case workspace combining structured data and PDF evidence
- Deterministic validation and exception rules
- Evidence-grounded summaries and drafted follow-up questions
- Human review and approval controls
- Role-based access and audit events
- PDF decision report and outbound webhook
- Keyboard-accessible, responsive interface
- Tests for rules, permissions, and the critical case journey

## Initial demonstration

The portfolio version will use fictional business-account onboarding data and three cases: a complete application, an application with missing ownership information, and an application containing conflicting values and an expired document.

## Candidate success measures

- Time required to identify and resolve exceptions
- Percentage of findings correctly detected by deterministic rules
- AI suggestions accepted, edited, or rejected by reviewers
- Number of reviewer actions required per case
- Task completion and error rates from usability testing
- Accessibility and automated-test results

## Open framing decisions

- Primary user and operating context
- Job to be done
- Current workflow and failure points
- Highest-risk assumptions
- Primary measurable outcome

## Working product statement

> Clear Case helps operations teams resolve incomplete onboarding cases by bringing rules, evidence, AI-assisted recommendations, and human decisions into one auditable workspace.
